# PennyPincher User Manual

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to PennyPincher, your personal finance management application. This document provides a comprehensive guide to using PennyPincher to effectively track your income, expenses, and overall financial health. This guide assumes that PennyPincher is already installed and running on your system.

## 2. The Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first screen you will see upon launching PennyPincher. It provides a high-level overview of your current financial status.

* **Current Balance:** This is a prominent display of your total funds, calculated by subtracting your total expenses from your total income.
* **Recent Income:** A list of your most recently added income transactions.
* **Recent Expenses:** A list of your most recently added expense transactions.
* **Navigation Menu:** Use the navigation menu, typically located on the side or top of the screen, to access the different sections of the application.

## 3. Managing Transactions

This section details how to add, edit, and delete your financial transactions.

### 3.1 Adding a New Transaction

To add a new transaction, whether it is an income or an expense:

1.  Navigate to the respective "Income" or "Expenses" section of the application.
2.  Locate and click the "Add New" or a similarly labeled button.
3.  A form will appear. Fill in the required details:
    * **Description:** A brief note about the transaction (e.g., "Monthly Salary," "Groceries").
    * **Amount:** The monetary value of the transaction.
    * **Date:** The date the transaction occurred.
    * **Category:** Assign the transaction to a category (see Section 4).
4.  Click "Save" or "Add" to record the transaction.

### 3.2 Editing an Existing Transaction

If you need to make changes to a previously entered transaction:

1.  Navigate to the "Income" or "Expenses" list.
2.  Locate the transaction you wish to edit.
3.  Click on the "Edit" icon or button associated with that transaction.
4.  The transaction's details will appear in a form. Modify the information as needed.
5.  Click "Save" or "Update" to apply the changes.

### 3.3 Deleting a Transaction

To permanently remove a transaction:

1.  Go to the "Income" or "Expenses" list.
2.  Find the transaction you wish to delete.
3.  Click on the "Delete" icon or button.
4.  A confirmation prompt will appear to prevent accidental deletion. Confirm your choice to remove the transaction.

## 4. Organizing with Categories

Categories help you group your transactions to better understand your spending and income habits.

### 4.1 Creating and Managing Categories

1.  Navigate to the "Categories" section.
2.  To add a new category, click the "Add New Category" button.
3.  Enter a name for the category (e.g., "Utilities," "Entertainment," "Freelance Work").
4.  Save the new category.
5.  From the "Categories" section, you can also edit the names of existing categories or delete them.

### 4.2 Assigning a Category to a Transaction

When you are adding or editing a transaction, you will see a "Category" dropdown menu. Select the most appropriate category from the list to assign it to the transaction.

## 5. Visualizing Your Finances

PennyPincher includes a "Graphs" or "Reports" section with visual representations of your financial data.

* **Balance Over Time:** A line graph that shows the progression of your account balance over a selected period.
* **Income vs. Expenses:** A bar or pie chart comparing your total income to your total expenses.
* **Spending by Category:** A pie or donut chart that breaks down your expenses by category, showing where your money is going.

You can often filter these graphs by date range to analyze specific periods.

## 6. Importing and Exporting Data

You can manage your data by importing from or exporting to CSV (Comma-Separated Values) files.

### 6.1 How to Import Data

1.  Navigate to the "Settings" or "Data" section.
2.  Select the "Import" option.
3.  Choose the CSV file from your computer that you wish to import. Ensure your CSV file is formatted correctly with columns that match PennyPincher's transaction fields (e.g., Date, Description, Amount, Category).
4.  Follow the on-screen prompts to complete the import process.

### 6.2 How to Export Data

1.  Navigate to the "Settings" or "Data" section.
2.  Select the "Export" option.
3.  Choose the data you wish to export (e.g., all transactions, income only, expenses only).
4.  A CSV file containing the selected data will be generated and downloaded to your computer.

## 7. Using the AI Assistant (Abe)

PennyPincher includes an AI-powered assistant named Abe to help you with your budgeting questions.

1.  Access Abe through the chat icon or link within the application.
2.  Type your question into the chat box. You can ask questions related to your budget, financial best practices, or how to use the application.
3.  Abe will provide a response based on the data within your PennyPincher application and its general knowledge base.
